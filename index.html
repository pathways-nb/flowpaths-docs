FlowPaths Documentation

Turn your Jira board's own issue history into flow metrics — cycle time, flow, aging work, and probabilistic delivery forecasts. No setup, no story points, no guessing.

What is FlowPaths?

FlowPaths is a Jira Cloud app that turns your board's issue history into flow-metrics analytics, using well-established methods: cycle time percentiles, cumulative flow, aging work-in-progress, and Monte Carlo forecasting. Every chart is read-only and builds itself from the statuses your issues actually moved through — nothing to configure to see your first chart, nothing for FlowPaths to write back to your Jira data.

The problem it solves

Most delivery conversations eventually land on two questions: "how predictable are we?" and "when will this actually be done?" Teams usually answer these with a mix of gut feel and story-point estimates that don't reliably hold up. FlowPaths answers them differently — using your team's own history, not story points or manual estimation exercises. It measures how work has actually flowed through your board, and uses that real distribution to say how consistent your delivery is and how confident a forecast can be.

Who it's for

  • Any Jira Software team working from a board, whether you run continuous flow or sprints — FlowPaths measures actual flow either way.
  • Team leads and PMs who need a predictability conversation grounded in real data instead of a felt sense of "we're usually pretty fast."
  • Anyone who wants a delivery-date forecast without asking engineers to estimate story points.
No setup required. Install FlowPaths, open it from any Software project with a board, and it reads your board's existing saved filter automatically. See Getting Started for your first five minutes with the app.
