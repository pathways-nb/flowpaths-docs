FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Does FlowPaths write anything back to Jira?

No. Every Jira call FlowPaths makes is read-only. It never creates, edits, or deletes issues, statuses, or workflows.

Does it only work with Kanban boards?

No — FlowPaths works with any Jira Software project board, whether your team runs continuous flow or sprints. It measures how work actually flowed through your statuses, which applies either way.

What data does FlowPaths access?

Issue status, type, and dates (created, resolved, updated) plus status-change history and board configuration — never assignee, reporter, comments, attachments, or any other user-identifying field. See the Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

Do I need to configure anything before using it?

No. FlowPaths reads your board's existing saved filter automatically and shows a chart immediately. Setting up Status Mapping is optional but recommended, since it's what makes cycle time accurate for your specific workflow.

My numbers look wrong — why?

This is almost always a Status Mapping issue. If a status is left Unmapped, or mapped to the wrong stage, cycle time and the Dashboard KPIs will be off. Open the "Doing → Done" pill and check every status on your board is mapped correctly.

Everything shows "1 day" even though work finishes much faster — why?

FlowPaths measures cycle time in whole calendar days by default — same-day work always reads as "1 day," whether it took 10 minutes or 20 hours. If your team's work regularly finishes within hours, try Duration mode — it switches to raw elapsed time instead, so fast items are no longer indistinguishable from each other.

Is my data shared with anyone outside Atlassian?

No. FlowPaths makes zero calls to any third party — no analytics, no external servers. It's eligible for Atlassian's "Runs on Atlassian" program, which specifically certifies apps with no external network egress.

What happens to my configuration if I uninstall FlowPaths?

Your Status Mapping and excluded-issue lists are stored in Atlassian's own Forge storage, not on any server of ours, and follow Atlassian's standard data-lifecycle/deletion process on uninstall. See Privacy Policy §7.

Can I use it across multiple projects or boards?

Yes. Status Mapping and exclusions are saved per project, so each project keeps its own configuration — switching projects in Jira automatically switches which configuration FlowPaths uses.

How is this different from just looking at the board?

The board shows you what's happening right now. FlowPaths measures how work has actually moved historically, and turns that into percentiles, trend signals, and forecasts — so conversations about predictability are grounded in data instead of a felt sense of speed.

What does FlowPaths cost?

FlowPaths has a free tier for smaller teams, with usage-based pricing beyond that. See the Pricing tab on the Atlassian Marketplace listing for current details.

Something's not working — how do I get help?

Email info@yourpaths.eu — this is also where you can report a bug or suggest an improvement.
